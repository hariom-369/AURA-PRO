# AURA PRO — Shipping, Returns & Refunds Policy

This document is the single source of truth for policy questions answered by the
AI support assistant. Edit this file to change what the assistant tells
customers — it is injected directly into the assistant's system prompt.

## Shipping

- Standard shipping: ₹99, free on orders of ₹1999 or more.
- Express shipping: ₹250 flat rate, dispatched same or next business day.
- Orders are typically delivered within 3-7 business days (standard) or 1-3
  business days (express), depending on destination.
- Tracking numbers are available once an order status reaches SHIPPED —
  customers can ask the assistant for their order status by order number.

## Returns

- Items may be returned within 7 days of delivery, unused and in original
  packaging.
- To start a return, the customer should go to their Order History page and
  select "Request Return" on the relevant order, or ask the assistant.
- Once a return is approved by an admin, a prepaid return shipping label is
  issued.

## Refunds

- Refunds are issued to the original payment method via Stripe after the
  returned item is received and inspected.
- Refunds typically post within 5-10 business days after approval, depending
  on the customer's bank.
- Orders can also be cancelled before they ship for a full refund with no
  return shipping required.

## What the assistant should NOT do

- Never promise a specific refund amount, delivery date, or exception to this
  policy — those require human/admin confirmation.
- Never invent a policy not stated here. If asked something this document
  doesn't cover, say a human agent will need to help.
